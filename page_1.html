Veg Biryani | Vegetable Biryani

Updated: December 15, 2025 | By Yashya

An aromatic rice dish made with basmati rice, mix veggies, herbs & biryani spices. This Vegetable Biryani is a total crowd-pleaser, perfect for everything from busy weeknights to festive family dinners.

About Veg Biryani

This recipe covers the One Pot Method—ideal for busy weeknights using a pressure cooker or pot—and the traditional Dum Method for when you want that authentic, slow-cooked restaurant taste.

Ingredients

  • Rice: 2 cups Basmati Rice (soaked for 30 mins)
  • Vegetables: Carrots, Peas, Beans, and Cauliflower (1 cup each)
  • Aromatics: 1 Large Onion (sliced), 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • Spices: 2 tsp Biryani Masala, 1 tsp Turmeric, 1 tsp Chili Powder
  • Herbs: Fresh Mint and Coriander leaves

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sauté Aromatics: Heat oil in a pot. Add whole spices (cloves, cardamom, cinnamon) and sliced onions until golden brown.
  2. Cook Veggies: Add the ginger-garlic paste and the mixed vegetables. Sauté for 5 minutes with the powdered spices.
  3. Layer Rice: Drain the soaked rice and spread it over the vegetable base.
  4. Simmer: Add 3.5 cups of water (or as needed), sprinkle mint and coriander, and close the lid.
  5. Dum: Cook on low heat for 15-20 minutes until the water is absorbed and rice is fluffy.
