Aromatic Egg Biryani (Ande ki Biryani)

This Egg Biryani is a delicious, protein-packed alternative to meat biryani. It features perfectly boiled eggs that are shallow-fried in spices and layered with fragrant Basmati rice.

Pro Tip: Always prick the boiled eggs with a fork before frying to prevent them from bursting and to let the masala seep inside!

Ingredients

For the Eggs:
  • 6 Hard-boiled eggs
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric & 1/2 tsp Kashmiri Red Chili powder
  • Salt to taste
For the Rice:
  • 2 cups Basmati Rice (washed and soaked for 30 mins)
  • Whole spices: 1 Bay leaf, 2 Cloves, 1 Star Anise
For the Gravy:
  • 2 Large Onions (thinly sliced)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • 1 cup Thick Curd (Yogurt)
  • 1 tsp Biryani Masala powder
  • Handful of Mint and Coriander leaves

Method

  1. Prepare Eggs: Heat 1 tbsp oil. Add turmeric, chili powder, and the boiled eggs. Sauté until the eggs get a golden, crispy skin. Set aside.
  2. Par-cook Rice: Boil water with whole spices and salt. Add rice and cook until it is 70% done (firm to the bite). Drain and set aside.
  3. Make Masala: In the same pot, fry onions until brown. Add ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, and spices. Stir in the yogurt and cook until oil separates.
  4. Layering: Place the fried eggs into the masala. Top with the par-cooked rice. Sprinkle mint, coriander, and optional saffron milk.
  5. The Dum: Cover the pot with a heavy lid or foil. Cook on very low heat for 12-15 minutes.
